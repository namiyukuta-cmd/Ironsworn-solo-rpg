# GM Rules — Public Mirror

Source: Google Sheets `GM_ENGINE`

Purpose: Generate forward motion and convergence. Do not solve stagnation by endlessly adding hooks.

- R001 Foreground cap — Keep at most 3 foreground story threads. Everything else is deferred/background.
- R002 No hook spam — Do not create a new major unresolved hook merely to make something happen. A new major hook must directly arise from the current consequence and also advance, close, or merge an existing foreground thread.
- R003 Meaningful beat test — A played beat must advance the objective, advance/resolve a foreground hook, impose a meaningful consequence, or reveal useful information. If none applies, compress/skip the beat.
- R004 Convergence trigger — After 2 meaningful beats with no objective progress and no hook advancement/closure, the next beat must converge via reveal, consequence, arrival, decision point, or closure.
- R005 Scene mystery cap — Keep at most 2 unresolved scene-local UNKNOWN items.
- R006 Canon lock — Established facts cannot be silently rewritten. Unfixed truths may remain undecided until needed. Once determined, lock and preserve them.
- R007 Reveal integrity — A major twist should connect to at least 2 established facts/clues when claiming earlier causality.
- R008 No infinite mastermind ladder — Do not reveal a higher hidden mastermind just to extend the story. A higher-level antagonist requires prior evidence or an already-established intermediary relationship.
- R009 Side-event rule — Side events must connect to a foreground thread or resolve within the same scene.
- R010 Current-vow priority — V004 must not be delayed by unrelated content. Once the gate beat resolves, compress toward the contact point unless a genuinely meaningful obstacle or discovery intervenes.
- R011 Oracle commitment — When several plausible branches exist and no established fact selects one, use an oracle/GM random choice instead of endlessly preserving options. If the result determines a truth, record and lock it.
- R012 Quest generation — New quests should emerge from consequences, revelations, NPC needs, or discovered opportunities at natural boundaries. Foreground at most 1 new major quest at a time.
- R013 Arc closure first — When an arc/vow completion condition is achieved, resolve its progress move/mechanics before launching the next major arc.
- R014 Player/GM boundary — Player exclusively controls White Gold's speech, voluntary actions, choices, thoughts and feelings. GM controls world, NPCs, events, mechanics and consequences.
- R015 Skip mundane transitions — Turn number is a log index, not a pacing unit. Routine travel, exits, meals and sleep are compressed unless meaningful.

Design principle: The system is a guardrail, not a prewritten plot. It should preserve surprise while forcing branches to merge, resolve, or become relevant.

Note: Current scene-specific FOCUS / CLUE / UNKNOWN / NEXT entries are intentionally not mirrored here because the repository is public and those can contain GM-only information.
